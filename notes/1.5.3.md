## EdgeSlider 1.5.3

- Fixes the sliders and on-screen indicator stopping after days of use. The trackpad connection is now rebuilt after sleep, wake and display changes.
- Brightness always targets the built-in display and no longer jumps to 0% when it cannot be read.
- A stuck brightness or volume call can no longer disable the controls until relaunch.
- The cursor is released if the trackpad stops reporting mid-gesture.
