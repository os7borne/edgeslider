## EdgeSlider 1.4

- New: Corner Position. Choose whether the corner double-taps (Quick Mute and Switch Output) use the top or the bottom corners of the trackpad. Find it under Settings.
- New: Hold Delay. The edge sliders now wait a moment (150 ms, adjustable in Settings) before engaging, so a palm brushing the edge no longer changes brightness or volume.
- Removed: the media strip (bottom-edge swipe to skip tracks).
