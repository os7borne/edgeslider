Volume and brightness, right at the edge of your trackpad.

EdgeSlider is a tiny menu bar app for Mac. Rest a finger on the left or right edge and slide. No keys, no menus, no reaching for the keyboard.

Three seconds to learn

The edges of the trackpad become sliders. Everything else on the pad works exactly as before.

Touch the edge

Put one finger down on the far left edge for brightness or the far right edge for volume.

Slide up or down

The level follows your finger, with a small click at every step and a bar on screen showing where you are.

Lift to finish

The bar slides away on its own. Your pointer never moved.

Small app, careful details

Built to stay out of your way until you reach for it.

Edge sliders

Left edge for brightness, right edge for volume. Swap them in one click if you prefer it the other way round.

Haptic feedback

Feel each step through the trackpad, with a firmer bump at the minimum and maximum. Off, Light or Strong.

Pointer stays put

While you slide, the cursor is pinned in place, so you never lose what you were pointing at.

Switch output Optional

Double-tap the top-left corner to cycle the sound through your speakers, headphones and displays. Off by default, for the same Tap to Click reason.

Quick mute Optional

Double-tap the top-right corner to mute. Off by default, because taps can also click when Tap to Click is on.

No accidental touches

A touch has to land on the edge and then move before it does anything, and you can limit it to the lower part of the pad.

Plus adjustable sensitivity and edge width, a show-percentage switch, launch at login and automatic updates. It lives in the menu bar and nowhere else.

Good to know

Why isn't it on the App Store?

To see your fingers on the trackpad edge, EdgeSlider uses Apple's undocumented multitouch interface, which the App Store doesn't allow. It's signed and notarized by Apple like any other download, so it opens without warnings. A future macOS update could change that interface and break the app.

Does it work with an external display?

Volume works with whatever output you use. Brightness changes the built-in display only.

How do I make it start at login?

Open the menu bar icon, choose Settings, and switch on Open at Login. For this to work reliably, keep EdgeSlider in your Applications folder.

Does it collect any data?

No. EdgeSlider has no accounts, no analytics and makes no network connections. Your settings are stored on your Mac.
